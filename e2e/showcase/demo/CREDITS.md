# Demo textures

The textures in `textures/` are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/textures) and are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no attribution is required, and it is given here anyway.

Each one is the 1k diffuse map of the Poly Haven asset, converted to JPEG at a lower quality with ffmpeg so that none is larger than about 200 KB. The seed uploads them under a Spanish display name.

| File | Poly Haven asset | Shown as | Used on |
|---|---|---|---|
| `red_brick_03.jpg` | `red_brick_03` | Ladrillo | Exterior walls |
| `wood_floor_deck.jpg` | `wood_floor_deck` | Madera | Floors of the living room and the bedroom |
| `white_plaster_02.jpg` | `white_plaster_02` | Yeso | Partitions, the street facade and the bedroom walls |
| `beige_wall_001.jpg` | `beige_wall_001` | Pintura | Living room walls and the roof |
| `floor_tiles_06.jpg` | `floor_tiles_06` | Baldosa | Bathroom floor |
| `concrete_wall_008.jpg` | `concrete_wall_008` | Cemento | Columns |

The house itself is `public/demo/house.glb`, built from the Kenney Building Kit (CC0), see `public/demo/LICENSE-kenney.txt`.
